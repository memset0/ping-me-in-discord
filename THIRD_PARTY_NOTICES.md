[`jdecked/twemoji`](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji) project through
jsDelivr by default.

The Twemoji project and its contributors are not affiliated with or responsible
for `notify-me-on-discord`.

No Twemoji artwork is embedded in this repository or its compiled binaries.
Downloaded artwork is stored in the current user's application data cache.
